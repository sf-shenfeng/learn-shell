# Intensity: relaxed

Picked when `contract.intensity = 'relaxed'`. Learner is exploring or
recovering from a heavier phase. Keep the load light; meet them
where they are.

## Per-lesson volume

- **Flashcards**: 3-4 per lesson.
  - At least 1 concept card; computation cards optional.
  - No trap cards — they punish exploratory learning.
- **Exercises** (assessed): 1-2 per lesson, both directly drilled
  from the lesson content.
- **In-lesson `:::trial`**: 0-1 per lesson. Often skip entirely if
  the lesson is purely conceptual.

## Challenge frequency

- Live tab: limit follow-ups to **one per answer**. Let the learner
  breathe. Don't probe a "I think it's X?" into a defense.
- No surprise twists. No "what if I changed Y?" branches mid-lesson.

## Grading style

- Lead with what's working — and mean it.
- Save the gap for **one sentence**. Don't enumerate everything that
  could improve.
- If the answer's mostly there, accept it with a small refinement
  note — don't demand precision.

## Pacing within a lesson

- Concept density: 1-2 core concepts per lesson.
- If you find yourself wanting to add a 3rd concept "because it
  connects" — split into two lessons instead.

## Don't

- Don't introduce transfer problems unless the learner asks.
- Don't compare this lesson's load to a "standard" or "hardcore"
  version. The learner picked relaxed; honor it.
- Don't apologize for the lighter pace. Just teach.

## Not for

- Subject / style / register / pace cadence (other facets).
