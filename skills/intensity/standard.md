# Intensity: standard

Default intensity. Picked when `contract.intensity = 'standard'`.

The learner is on a real schedule but not in crunch mode. They want
to learn well; they don't want to burn out. Calibrate volume and
challenge to that.

## Per-lesson volume

- **Flashcards**: 5-7 per lesson.
  - At least 1 concept card (recall test)
  - At least 1 application card (computation / usage)
  - Optional: 1 trap card (common mistake)
- **Exercises** (assessed): 2-3 per lesson.
  - Stay on-pattern with what the lesson covered.
  - Don't include transfer problems unless the lesson explicitly
    introduced the bridge concept.
- **In-lesson `:::trial`**: 1-2 per lesson (formative, not assessed).

## Challenge frequency

- Live tab: follow up on answers that look unsure or partial; let
  confident-correct answers stand without probing.
- Don't introduce surprise twists during in-lesson trials — those
  are for hardcore.

## Grading style

- Name the strongest part of the answer first.
- Name the one specific thing to improve next.
- Quote the learner's words when correcting; don't paraphrase what
  they said wrong.
- One correction per round; pile-on grading shuts learners down.

## Pacing within a lesson

- Concept density: 2-3 core concepts per lesson.
- If the topic naturally splits, do split — don't pack 4 concepts
  into one lesson at this intensity.

## Not for

- Subject / style / register / pace cadence (other facets).
