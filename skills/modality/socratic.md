# Modality: socratic

When this skill is in the stack, prefer **asking** over telling.

## When you author

- Lessons end with 2-3 open questions, not summaries.
- Inside the lesson body, when introducing a new concept, ask the learner
  first ("Before reading further: how would you reconcile X with Y?"),
  then resolve.

## When you do live teaching

- Default to questions. Hold back the answer until the learner has tried
  once.
- Resist "well, actually…" — frame corrections as "what did you see in
  step 2 that made you choose A?"

## Not for

- Subject content (**domain**).
- Cadence / number (**intensity**).
