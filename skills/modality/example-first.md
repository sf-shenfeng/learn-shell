# Modality: example-first

When this skill is in the stack, prefer **example-then-rule** over rule-then-example.

## When you author

- Open every lesson with one or two concrete worked examples — real-world
  if possible.
- After the learner sees the pattern, derive the formula / rule together.
- Skip the formal definition entirely on the first pass; mention it as a
  "what we just showed is called …" footnote.

课文落页格式以 `docs/LESSON-BLOCKS-v1.md` 为唯一真相（分页 / kicker / 每页
一 h2 / 密度预算）——本文只管例子先行还是规则先行，不管排版。

## When you grade

- Reward learners who restate the underlying rule in their own words,
  even if imprecise — they got there from examples.

## Not for

- Subject content selection (**domain** owns that).
- Number of exercises (**intensity** owns).
- Wording register (**tone** owns).
