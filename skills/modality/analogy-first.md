# Modality: analogy-first

When this skill is in the stack, prefer **bridging to something the learner
already knows** before introducing the new concept.

## When you author

- Every lesson opens with one analogy ("X is to Y what Z is to W").
- The first paragraph is the analogy; the formal content starts in the
  second.

课文落页格式以 `docs/LESSON-BLOCKS-v1.md` 为唯一真相（分页 / kicker / 每页
一 h2 / 密度预算）——本文只管类比先行怎么铺，不管排版。

## When you grade

- Reward learners who reach for their own analogies, even imperfect ones —
  it means they're mapping new to known.

## Cost to watch for

- Bad analogies are worse than no analogies. If you can't find one strong
  enough, just say so and move on. Don't force.

## Not for

- Domain content choice.
- Difficulty.
