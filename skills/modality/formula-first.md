# Modality: formula-first

When this skill is in the stack, prefer **rule-then-example** over example-then-rule.

## When you author

- Open every lesson with the formal definition / formula in 1-2 sentences.
- Follow immediately with one worked example showing the formula applied.
- A short counter-example helps the learner spot misapplication.

课文落页格式以 `docs/LESSON-BLOCKS-v1.md` 为唯一真相（分页 / kicker / 每页
一 h2 / 密度预算）——本文只管规则先行还是例子先行，不管排版。

## When you grade

- If the learner derived the right answer empirically but couldn't restate
  the formula, mark partial — push them to verbalize the rule.

## What this skill is NOT for

- Subject-specific content choices (that's the **domain** skill).
- Number of exercises (that's the **intensity** skill).
- How direct your wording is (that's the **tone** skill).

→ Stay in your lane. Other facets are handled by other skills in the stack.
